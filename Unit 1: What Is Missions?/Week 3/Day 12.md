# Day 12: How does William Carey's Story Apply to Us?

In Unit 2, the focus will be on local missions. While William Carey moved to another country to spread the Gospel, making disciples of Christ is something we can do locally. 

Read over the [Day 12 Activity](https://github.com/RedBirdEducation/Bible_LiveOnMission/blob/main/Unit%201%3A%20What%20Is%20Missions%3F/Week%203/Day%2012%20Activity.pdf), brainstorm some ideas for how you can be a part of the local harvest, and pick your top 3 ideas. You will do one of these as part of Unit 2.

### Assignments:
1. Recite or write this week's memory verse for a quiz grade.
2. Take the quiz at the top of [this page](https://torchlighters.org/kids-activities/william-carey/). Retake it as needed until you understand the material.
