# Day 9: Introduction to William Carey

So far, we've looked at how the Bible defines missions--that is the most important part! We've also looked at how the Apostle Paul was the first missionary.

Now, we'll look at **William Carey**, the man who is often called **the Father of Modern Missions**.

Despite his title, Carey was not the first missionary after Bible times. For instance, St. Patrick was a missionary to Ireland in the 5th century, and John Eliot and David Brainerd were missionaries to the Native Americans in the mid-17th and mid-18th centuries, respectively. However, by the late 18th century when Carey came along, Christians did not see missions as a priority.

William Carey began his life working as a shoemaker, but when he became convinced that the Great Commission was applicable to all believers and not just the original apostles, he decided to take action. He wrote a book called _An Enquiry into the Obligations of Christians_, helped found the first modern missionary society, and worked for 41 years in India sharing the Gospel and translating Scripture.

Carey had a famous quote:
> "Expect great things from God; attempt great things for God.”

This week, we'll learn more about William Carey and memorize a verse that reminds us that God can do greater things than we could ever imagine and can give us the power to glorify Him.
