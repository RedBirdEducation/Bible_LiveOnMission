# Day 7: Paul's Audience

The Great Commission tells us to make disciples of all nations. This doesn't mean we should make every country have a Christian government; it means that we should tell *everyone everywhere* about the hope that's in Christ. Read **1 Corinthians 9:19-23**, where Paul wrote about how he took great lengths to share the Gospel with all different kinds of people. 

One story from Paul's second missionary journey that especially highlights that *everyone* is in need of the Gospel is from his time in Philippi. Watch the video below, and then you'll go deeper into the story during today's assignment:</br>
[Philippi - Footsteps of the Apostle Paul](https://youtu.be/bYuCXw9hgno?si=9DoM_rlltNqkMjC_)

### Assignments:
1. Write this week's memory verse 2 times:
> "That is why we never give up. Though our bodies are dying, our spirits are being renewed every day. For our present troubles are small and won’t last very long. Yet they produce for us a glory that vastly outweighs them and will last forever!" - 2 Corinthians 4:16-17

2. Read Acts 16:10-40. Then, make a [Venn diagram](https://lucid.co/diagram/venn-diagram/tutorial), either on paper or in an electronic program) showing the similarities and difference between the 3 people with whom God shared the Gospel in Philippi (i.e., Lydia, the slave girl, the jailer). Some things you might want to consider are whether or not each was wealthy or powerful, if the person worshipped God already, if the person was a Jew or a Gentile.
