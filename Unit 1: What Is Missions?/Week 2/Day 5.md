# Day 5: Introduction to Paul

Have you heard of the Apostle Paul in the Bible? Chances are, you have. Paul is notable because he wrote much of the New Testament, but he is also notable because he is the first missionary in the Bible.

**Start by reading Acts 9:1-31 and Acts 11:19-30.** These passages tell us about how Paul became a believer and how Barnabas invited him to get involved with missions. 

Now, watch this video to learn more about Paul's missionary journeys as documented in the book of Acts: </br>
[Book of Acts Summary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z-17KxpjL0Q&t=1s)

This week, we're going to look at Paul's ministry: how he endured persecution for sharing the gospel, how he reached a diverse group of people, and how his ministry was only made possible through God's grace. To start off the week, complete today's assignments.
