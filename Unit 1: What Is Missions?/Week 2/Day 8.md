# Day 8: The Central Theme of Paul's Ministry

Paul led an amazing ministry, sharing the Gospel all over the Roman empire, and ultimately, he was martyred for his faith. However, Paul's ministry was not something he did out of his own power. In Romans 15:16-18, Paul wrote: </br>
> "I am a special messenger from Christ Jesus to you Gentiles. I bring you the Good News so that I might present you as an acceptable offering to God, made holy by the Holy Spirit. So I have reason to be enthusiastic about all Christ Jesus has done through me in my service to God. Yet I dare not boast about anything except what Christ has done through me, bringing the Gentiles to God by my message and by the way I worked among them."
