# Day 1: Introduction

What do you think missions is? More importantly, what does God say about missions in the Bible? That's the question we want to answer this week and, for that matter, throughout this course. 

Let's start the course with a quick game (hint: you can play solo mode): </br>
[Missions Quiz Game](https://create.kahoot.it/details/75e583de-da4c-444e-8b9e-359488aab723)

Now, read Matthew 28:19-20:
> "Therefore, go and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit. Teach these new disciples to obey all the commands I have given you. And be sure of this: I am with you always, even to the end of the age."

This verse is known as the **Great Commission** and is where Jesus commanded His followers to spread the Gospel. We were not there when Jesus gave this command, but God put it in the Bible so that we (His followers now) could read it and obey as well.
