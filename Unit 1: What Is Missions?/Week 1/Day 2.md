# Day 2: More in the Bible on Missions

Yesterday, you learned about the Great Commission. Today, let's look at what else the Bible says about missions. Find the following verses in your Bible and read them:
* Acts 1:8
* Romans 10:9-15
* Philippians 1:3-20

Let's look a bit deeper at Acts 1:8 and some of the verses after it: 
> "But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes upon you. And you will be my witnesses, telling people about me everywhere—in Jerusalem, throughout Judea, in Samaria, and to the ends of the earth. After saying this, he was taken up into a cloud while they were watching, and they could no longer see him. As they strained to see him rising into heaven, two white-robed men suddenly stood among them. 'Men of Galilee,' they said, 'why are you standing here staring into heaven? Jesus has been taken from you into heaven, but someday he will return from heaven in the same way you saw him go!'" - Acts 1:8-11

These verses are what is called Christ's **ascension**, when He was taken back up to heaven after resurrecting from the dead. Notice, Jesus didn't tell the disciples, "Oh, I saved you, so now, go enjoy your life!" Instead, He told them to tell people about Him *everywhere*, and as soon as He left, the angels told the disciples to get busy!
