# Day 4: Missions Defined (Cont.)

This week, you've looked at what the Bible says about missions, and you've heard from a missionary about missions. Now, it's time to put into words what missions is:
> "Missions is the sending of and partnering with qualified workers, often across linguistic, geographical, religious, and cultural boundaries for the purpose of making disciples, planting churches, and spreading the gospel with all people." - Jason Sneed

Here's what some of that means:
* Linguistic boundaries - different languages
* Geographical boundaries - different places in the world
* Religious boundaries - different beliefs about God / gods
* Cultural boundaries - different ways of living everyday life

At the core of mission is **spreading the gospel with all people**, and that's something you can do anywhere, regardless if the above boundaries are present or not.
