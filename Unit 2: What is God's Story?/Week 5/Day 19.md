# Day 19: Why Did God Allow Sin?

When Adam and Eve sinned, sin entered the world, and every human (except Jesus) since has been born with a sin nature. As a result, we live in a world marked by the existence of evil and the sobering reality of its effects. Even non-Christians realize that there is evil all around us, and this prevents many people from believing in God, for they wonder how a good God could allow so much pain and suffering. 

Let's take today to ask why God allowed sin in the first place. Explore the topic by reading the following article and watching the included video: ["If God knew that Adam and Eve would sin, why did He create them?"](https://www.gotquestions.org/if-God-knew-Adam-Eve-sin.html)

The author of the above article points out valid (and biblically true) arguments that God is sovereign yet gives us the choice of whether or not to obey Him and that God's ultimate goal is His glory. However, these arguments still do not explain _why_ God chose to design the universe the way He did, with sin existing in the first place for us to choose. God's ultimate answer to the problem of evil is found in Isaiah 55:8-9:
> “My thoughts are nothing like your thoughts,” says the Lord.
    “And my ways are far beyond anything you could imagine.
For just as the heavens are higher than the earth,
    so my ways are higher than your ways
    and my thoughts higher than your thoughts."
