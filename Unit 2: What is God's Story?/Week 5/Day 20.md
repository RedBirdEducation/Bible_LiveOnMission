# Day 20: Are We Without Hope?

Man sinned, and as a result, almost every person born afterwards has had a sin nature. This sin separates mankind from God. How, then, can we ever have hope to fellowship with God again?

Well, even in Genesis 3 (The Fall), God was already planning to save us from the mess we made. Read God's curse to the serpent again:
> "And I will cause hostility between you and the woman, and between your offspring and her offspring. He will strike your head, and you will strike his heel.” - Genesis 3:15

This verse, known as the **protoevangelium** (meaning "first gospel"), is the first time God promises that He will save us from our sins, and He said that our Savior would be injured on His heel before defeating Satan.

Read **Romans 5:1-21** to learn more about how hope for salvation from our sins is found in Jesus. Then, watch the following video: [The Truth About What REALLY Saves You](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P8CcrvA3Ffc).
