# Day 18: What's the Big Deal?

We learned yesterday how sin entered the world. Today, we're going to look at the effects of sin on mankind.

In Genesis 3:16-19, God laid out man and woman's punishment for disobeying:
>  Then he said to the woman,
“I will sharpen the pain of your pregnancy,
    and in pain you will give birth.
And you will desire to control your husband,
    but he will rule over you.”
And to the man he said,
“Since you listened to your wife and ate from the tree
    whose fruit I commanded you not to eat,
the ground is cursed because of you.
    All your life you will struggle to scratch a living from it.
It will grow thorns and thistles for you,
    though you will eat of its grains.
By the sweat of your brow
    will you have food to eat
until you return to the ground
    from which you were made.
For you were made from dust,
    and to dust you will return.”

We see that several consequences resulted from sin:
* Increased pain in childbirth
* A power struggle between husband and wife
* A struggle against the earth for food and work
* Death (or a return to dust)

However, these physical consequences and the banishment from the Garden of Eden are just the tip of the iceberg. Looking back in Genesis 3:8, we see that God once walked in the garden with Adam and Eve; there was a direct fellowship between the Creator and His creation. The breaking of this direct relationship with God was the greatest consequence of sin.
