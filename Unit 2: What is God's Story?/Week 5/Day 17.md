# Day 17: The Fall of Man - The Problem in the Story

Often, one of the most difficult parts about sharing the Gospel is convincing people that they are sinners. Nobody likes to hear that he or she is not good, but the Bibel states this truth plainly. This week, we're going to take a look at how sin entered the world, why it's a problem, and how this sets the stage for a Savior. 

Let's start by reading what God tells us about the fall in **Genesis 3:1-24**.
