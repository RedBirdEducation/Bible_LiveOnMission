# Day 14: Adam and Eve in the Garden

At the conclusion of Genesis 1, we see that God made man in His own image:
> "So God created human beings in his own image. In the image of God he created them; male and female he created them." - Genesis 1:27

Why exactly does that matter though? Why are humans so different from the rest of God's creation, and why does the rest of God's story feature the relationship between Him and mankind? Watch the video and read the article at the following link to learn more: [What does it mean when God said, “Let Us make man in Our image” (Genesis 1:26)?](https://www.gotquestions.org/let-Us-make-man-in-Our-image.html)

Now, read Genesis 2 to learn about the Garden of Eden and how God created the first woman.
