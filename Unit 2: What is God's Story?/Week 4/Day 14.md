# Day 14: Why Is Creation Important?

You read yesterday about how God created the earth. Why is creation such an important part of God's story though? Why can't we just skip straight to the Fall, Redemption, and Restoration when talking about God's story? 

Read the following article and watch the attached video to learn some of the reasons Genesis 1 is so important: [Why Does Creation Matter?](https://answersingenesis.org/why-does-creation-matter/)

In the article, it mentions controversy over the age of the earth. This is one of many controversies _among Christians_ that challenge a 7-day, literal creation. Learn more about some of these challenges (and the Biblical answers) by reading the following article: [What's Wrong with "Progressive Creation"?](https://answersingenesis.org/creationism/old-earth/whats-wrong-with-progressive-creation/) </br>
Be sure to read the Scripture passages that the article references!
