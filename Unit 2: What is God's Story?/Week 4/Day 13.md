# Day 13: The Beginning of God's Story

Before you (or any missionary) can share God's story with others, you must know what God's story, the **Gospel**, is and how to communicate it effectively. This story can be split into 4 major "chapters":
1. **Creation** - God created the heavens and the earth and everything in them, including mankind, whom He created in His own image.
2. **Fall** - When the first man and woman disobeyed God's command, sin and death entered the world and separated mankind from the Holy God.
3. **Redemption** - Out of mercy, grace, and love, God sent His only Son down to earth to live a perfect life, die on a cross, and rise again to provide a way for mankind to be justified in God's sight. 
4. **Restoration** - One day, Jesus will return to conquer sin once and for all and establish a new heaven and a new earth.

This week, the focus will be on creation. As always, what the Bible says in more important than anything else. Let's start, then, by reading **Genesis 1:1-31**.
